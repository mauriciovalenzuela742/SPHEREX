**What you need to know to avoid repeating mistakes (verified on Nov 14):**
- **There are two DR1 «cubes» and only one works for Aladin.** The HiPS
  `https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/hips/SPHEREx/spherex_allsky_cube` does load: PNG, order 5, 102
  planes, 12.9″ pixel, open CORS. The version SIA2 delivers (`spherex_dr1_cube`, FITS of ~320 MB
  per tile) is useful neither for Aladin nor for cutouts. *The EDA said at first that the cube was not useful
  for Aladin: it was a mistake from testing only one path. It is already corrected in `docs/09`.*
- The HiPS `properties` has **the exact λ of each plane** in `data_cube_crval3_arr` (from 0.753 to
  4.988 µm), and the index rule: `plano = 17·(banda−1) + (canal−1)`. **Planes 50 and 51 come
  empty** (dichroic channels). Use it to label the slider; do not compute it.
- The cube is a **mosaic of all epochs**: it has no time axis and a moving object comes out shifted or double.
  It is coarser than the native images (12.9″/px against 6.15″/px).
- They are **stretched PNGs, without fluxes**: the band chart cannot come from the cube; it comes from the level 2
  images (with Bryan) and carries the contract's `caveat`.
- **IRSA does not respond with CORS outside the HiPS** (SIA2 and files): everything else goes through the backend.
- **The coordinates in `src/content/campos.ts` and the default value of `url.ts` are J2000**, even though
  the comment in `campos.ts` says «propagated». As of 2026 the objects are at: WISE 0855 **212″**,
  Barnard **270″**, eps Ind B 122″. With a 0.1° cutout (half side = 180″), **WISE 0855 and Barnard
  fall outside** once the blink is connected. In Aladin, the reticle marks a place where the object
  is not. The file is Mauricio's (C): let him know; Bryan's `/api/presets` already delivers the propagated
  positions.
- The linear propagation in `eda/comun.py` (`posicion_en`) matches what was measured in eps Ind B
  (−0.85/−0.71 px measured against −0.92/−0.57 px predicted). It serves as a reference for porting it to TS.
- `ponerPlano` uses a trick: Aladin 3.8.2 chooses the plane linearly in frequency, so the λ of the
  center of the desired plane is passed. **It is not verified that it lands on the correct plane in all bands.**
- Console: an `ERR_BLOCKED_BY_CLIENT` error + «Failed to fetch» comes from the usage log that Aladin
  sends to the CDS. It breaks nothing.
- `eps Ind B` (uppercase) is the pair of brown dwarfs; `eps Ind b` is a planet at 6.7′.

**What NOT to do:**
- Load in Aladin the FITS version of the cube that SIA2 delivers.
- Compute the λ of the planes by hand: it comes in `properties`.
- Use the cube for the spectrum or for showing motion (mosaic without time and without fluxes).
- Edit `Visor.tsx`, `url.ts` or `campos.ts` without agreeing with their owners (Antonia and Mauricio).
- Change the contract in `docs/03_ARQUITECTURA.md` without first changing the document and announcing it.
