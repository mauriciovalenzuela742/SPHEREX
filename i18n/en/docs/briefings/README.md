# Briefings

Two narrative summaries of the project, meant to be read (or listened to) straight through.
They are the quick way for someone on the team to arrive with context without having to read
the eleven numbered documents in `docs/`.

> **Note:** the briefings were written before the Nov 14 EDA. Where they differ from
> [`09_EDA_HALLAZGOS.md`](../09_EDA_HALLAZGOS.md) or [`10_REPARTO_DE_TRABAJO.md`](../10_REPARTO_DE_TRABAJO.md),
> those two rule (e.g.: the cube does not give the spectrum; WISE 0855 is a mandatory case and looks faint in a single exposure;
> the team is six; the backend is still to be decided). **The audios already generated with NotebookLM are
> outdated on those points** and would have to be regenerated.

| Briefing | What it covers | For whom |
|---|---|---|
| [`01_el_desafio_y_los_datos.md`](01_el_desafio_y_los_datos.md) | What the challenge asks for, why it is a viewer, the WiseView precedent, SPHEREx and its linear variable filter, where the data live, what is inside a file, Data Release 1 and the five test fields | Everyone, and especially the data and science roles |
| [`02_estrategia_equipo_y_pitch.md`](02_estrategia_equipo_y_pitch.md) | The eight suggestions as a rubric, the two architecture paths, the API contract, the four roles, how to use agents, the plan by blocks, the risks, why not video models, the jury and the pitch | Everyone, and especially the viewer and narrative roles |

November 14, 2026.
